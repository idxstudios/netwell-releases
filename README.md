# Netwell

Your net worth from your own statements, in the menu bar.

Netwell is a macOS menu bar app. Drop in your bank and brokerage statements and it reads them on your Mac, through your own Claude account, then tracks your net worth between statements: stocks and ETFs at market prices, bonds and GICs with their interest, cash as the statement printed it.

## Download

**[Download Netwell for Mac (Apple silicon)](https://github.com/idxstudios/netwell-releases/releases/latest/download/Netwell.dmg)**

1. Open `Netwell.dmg` and drag **Netwell** into **Applications**.
2. Open Netwell from Applications. It lives in your menu bar (⌥⌘N opens it).
3. Follow the short setup and connect your Claude account.

Netwell is signed and notarized by Apple (IDX Studios Inc.), so it opens without warnings. Installed copies update themselves.

## Requirements

- macOS 12 or later on Apple silicon (M1 or later)
- [Claude Code](https://docs.anthropic.com/claude-code) installed and signed in, on a Claude Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise plan

## Privacy

Your statements and numbers stay on your Mac. To read a statement, Netwell sends it to Claude using your own plan. Details are in the app under Profile › About & privacy.

Netwell gives information about your own statements, not financial advice.
